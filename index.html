Home Hub

A personal home automation project by Campbell Myott

Home Hub is Campbell Myott's personal, single-user home automation app. It links Google Assistant to his own Home Assistant instance so that he can control his own home by voice.

What it does

When Campbell speaks to Google Assistant, Home Hub passes that request on to his Home Assistant instance, which then operates the lights, switches and other devices in his own home. Home Hub connects to Google using Campbell's own Google account, through the Google Assistant SDK.

Who it is for

Home Hub is not a commercial product and is not offered to other users. It has one user, who is also its developer. There is no sign-up, no public service and no other accounts. The Google Cloud project behind it exists only so that Campbell can authorise his own account.

Privacy

Home Hub only handles the data it needs to relay Campbell's own commands. It does not share, sell or advertise with any data. Read the full privacy policy.
